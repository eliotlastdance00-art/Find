# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part in this project — in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions —
should be able to do so without harassment, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, language, appearance, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

* Be respectful and patient; assume good intentions.
* Give and accept constructive feedback; criticise ideas, not people.
* Write in any of the project languages (Turkmen, Russian, English) and help others understand.
* Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

* Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and trolling.
* Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome advances.
* Publishing other people's private information without permission.
* **Sharing manipulated media of real people** made to deceive, humiliate or harm them, or asking
  for help to make detection fail on such media. This project exists to expose forgeries.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, commits and other contributions that break these rules, and
may block people who repeat or escalate such behaviour. To report a problem, contact the
maintainer [@eliotlastdance00-art](https://github.com/eliotlastdance00-art) through GitHub, or use
the *Report content* option next to the comment. Reports are handled confidentially.
